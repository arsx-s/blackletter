# BlackLetter — Roadmap

BlackLetter is built as a layered research operating system. Each major version adds one engine to the product at a time, so that every release ships a complete, working surface before the next one begins.

## At a glance

| Version | Focus | State |
| --- | --- | --- |
| v1.0 | Learning Engine | Completed |
| v2.0 | Research Workspace | Completed |
| v3.0 | Knowledge Engine | Completed |
| v4.0 | Research Canvas | In Development |
| v5.0 | Offline Intelligence | Planned |

## v1.0 — Learning Engine (Completed)

The personal learning engine ships with version one: adaptive learning modes, structured study flows, and review mechanics that turn a topic into a mastered subject. Introduced the core hands-on experience of BlackLetter — a prompt becomes a structured, report-style document.

## v2.0 — Research Workspace (Completed)

Research sessions become living documents. Sources are captured with each claim, sessions preserve context end-to-end, and the work closes out as a durable research document you can reopen, revise, and reuse long after the conversation ends.

## v3.0 — Knowledge Engine (Completed)

The knowledge graph goes live. Documents feed into the graph as you research, concepts are linked and searchable, and relationship data is surfaced across the whole library — so a single finding pulls in everything connected to it. Knowledge Engine was the current release: **v3.0.0**.

## v4.0 — Research Canvas (In Development)

A visual knowledge canvas for working with ideas, graphs, and documents spatially. Move through concepts on a board, arrange notes freely, and let the structure of a research project take shape visually.

## v5.0 — Offline Intelligence (Planned)

The entire intelligent workload goes local. Research, learning, and knowledge operations run without a connection, so a session is never interrupted and the private-by-default model becomes completely offline-capable.

## Notes

- State in this table reflects the current public release (v3.0). Roadmap states are updated whenever a version is released or re-prioritized.
- Design and interaction standards are covered in `docs/Design-System.md`; system structure is documented in `docs/product-architecture.md`.
